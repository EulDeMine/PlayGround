THE SHORT VERSION

A little city, a little snake.

  • Take turns moving one square with the arrow keys or on-screen pad. In multiplayer, each snake gets one move per round.
  • Collect ✦ coins for cash and a longer tail. Don't run into yourself, another snake, or the city edge.
  • Buy property when you land on a for-sale tile. Passing your own places pays rent; visiting someone else's place pays them rent.
  • Use the stairways to zip across town. Reach $300 or finish all 24 rounds to decide the winner.
